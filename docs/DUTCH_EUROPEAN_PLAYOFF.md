# Dutch European ticket careers

The source connects the four-club European-ticket playoff to the dated career
stream, watched/quick matches, snapshots, backup validation and annual UEFA
admissions. The winner receives the Conference League qualifying berth. Saved
foreign Dutch domestic simulations also play and retain the full bracket;
they are explicitly career outcomes, not real results.

The KNVB regulation dated 1 August 2026 supplies cup-winner pass-down,
first-versus-fourth and second-versus-third semifinals, higher-finisher hosting,
extra time and shootouts, and provisional 27/30 May 2027 windows:
https://www.knvb.nl/downloads/bestand/30196/reglement-play-off-europees-ticket-seizoen-2026-27

Later dates and access lists are projections. Career dates can move for recovery
around a club’s European commitments. Existing UD1 admission archives retain
their original interpretation; new UD2 archives require a complete saved Dutch
playoff. A managed Dutch career never substitutes a foreign simulation for its
actual results. Unresolved final-table disciplinary/lot outcomes still require
the remaining national ranking work before a berth can be awarded.

Core checks cover every cup-winner position, semifinal/final home rights,
extra time/shootouts, lowest-seed victories, exact mid-bracket/completed reloads,
saved recovery dates and corrupt-input rejection. Annual access tests include
all ten complete national cup worlds, including the 802-club French world that
exceeded the previous registry bound during Android QA run 38093205458.
