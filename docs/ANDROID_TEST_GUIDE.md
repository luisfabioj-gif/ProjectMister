# BOSS XI Android real-world test

Install `BOSS-XI-v2.3.17-Match-Flow-Refinements.apk`, version 2.3.17 / code 43,
from the final chat handoff. [The build record](V2_3_17_CHANGES.md) identifies
its tested source, Android verification, signature and checksum. This update
refines physical pass receptions, close-range challenges and compact half-time
stats while retaining the stadium presentation and management features.

## Install and keep your saves

1. In the installed game, open career backup tools and export your careers first.
2. Install the delivered APK as an update. Keep the existing installation and
   its saves. If Android reports an incompatible signature, use the exported
   backup before changing installations.
3. Open an existing save and check your manager, squad, budget and completed
   result. The development gate tests upgrades and restoration across keys.

## Try the match-flow changes

- Watch short passes and crosses. The ball keeps its released destination;
  receivers must reach it before taking control. An unreachable pass remains
  loose until a player recovers it.
- Check that a distant defender closes down rather than being awarded a remote
  tackle. Match contribution records include successful close challenges.
- Open half-time tactics in landscape. Each starter shows current ability and
  condition beneath the numbered identity. Check all seven substitutes and
  change formation, then select **Start second half**. Both teams reposition
  before the kickoff resumes.
- Save and switch between careers in different countries. Club crests keep
  their original colours and initials while the next career loads.

## Try the presentation

- Use the bottom Home, Squad, Tactics, Inbox and More destinations.
- In Squad, use All / GK / DEF / MID / ATT and open a player portrait card.
- In Tactics, check that the pitch shows your selected XI and tap a marker to
  open that player's assignment. Auto-picked and manually assigned XIs work.
- Open the new fixture hero, profiles, club accounts, stadium and scouting pages.
- Play a watched match, check the lime score card and open statistics/tactics.

Existing saves are compatible with the UI update. You do not need to start a
new career just for the new appearance.

## Start the expanded game

Create a new career in an empty slot to get the revised club colours, observed
nationality mixes, calibrated starting budgets and strength, expanded country
world, national cups, three UEFA competitions and saved calendar from the
beginning. New careers offer twenty division choices; the Classic option has been
removed. Budgets and strength are explicitly labelled game estimates based on
public squad valuations. Players and portraits remain fictional.
Existing careers retain their stable club/player identities and original world;
an older smaller world is not silently replaced by the expanded one. Its next
edition adopts the newer calendar when the existing UEFA career rolls forward.

Choose either division in any of the ten countries. In Transfers, search other
countries/divisions and the unattached pool. Profiles explain the registration
decision for the current date. Windows and ordinary expiry exceptions differ
by country. Players retain stable club identities with edited display names; portraits are
fictional.

Play one watched match, visit half-time tactics, substitute, finish, save and
reload. Try one quick match. Check Calendar, league zones and History. Continue
through cups, European qualification and a season transition. A second-division
career can reach the top division without replacing its club or players.

Fixtures, future draws, foreign domestic outcomes and later editions are career
simulations. The History screen identifies its sourced historical records
separately from your own winners. Historical pages show seasons and winner
names; research references are retained in repository notes.

## Try the feedback changes

- Open a player or transfer profile and tap **Scout player**. Advance seven
  calendar days, then open the returned Inbox assessment or the Scout Centre.
  Check the current ability, development potential, strengths, costs and risk.
- In **Stadium & facilities**, tap a stand or building on the complex view.
  Approve an affordable project and advance through its construction period.
  Its image and level should change after completion. Save and reload it.
- Check **Match Summary** after a watched match and a quick result. The two
  teams have aligned comparison rows; detail not collected for quick results
  is displayed as unavailable.
- Check that league positions read **1st**, **2nd**, **3rd** and remain unique
  when teams share points. Open History to see the year and past winner rows.

## Report issues

Use your Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra with the normal display size first. Include
the app version, club/division, season/date, screen, action, expected result and
what happened. Say whether the match was watched or quick. A screenshot or short
recording and an exported career backup help reproduce progression/save issues.
For audio issues, include the selected volume and whether Bluetooth was active.

The development gate covers Android 35/36 emulators, compact landscape,
navigation, matches, recurring careers, backups and release installation.
Your physical phone test and Play Store publication are separate steps.
