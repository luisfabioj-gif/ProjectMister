# Stadium complex artwork

Five new original architectural images were created with the image-generation tool for the October 2026 device-feedback release. They depict an illustrative club complex, not a photograph of a named club's actual ground.

Files: `app/src/main/assets/stadium/complex-level-1.webp` through `complex-level-5.webp`. Source images were 1536 × 1024 PNG; runtime copies are 1280 × 853 WebP, quality 85, to keep Android memory and package size bounded.

Creative brief: a realistic miniature football complex seen from a low bird's-eye camera at roughly forty degrees. The stadium occupies the left side, with a correctly marked pitch and four separate stands. Two training pitches, training and medical buildings, gym, academy, shop, hospitality and restaurant buildings, fan plaza, parking, media centre and museum occupy fixed plots to the right and foreground. Use neutral materials, daylight, legible architectural changes, and no text, club logos or watermarks. Preserve the camera, pitch, roads, plot boundaries and building positions between stages.

| Stage | Architectural treatment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Modest single-tier stands and basic club buildings |
| 2 | Expanded roofs, seating and modernised facility facades |
| 3 | Two-tier stands and larger professional facilities |
| 4 | Premium two-tier stands, hospitality and developed campus |
| 5 | Elite three-tier stadium sections, terraces and advanced complex |

The later stages were generated by editing the preceding/reference image to preserve the layout. Runtime rendering selects each plot from its saved stage. Selection outlines, construction hatching, level indicators, cropping, panning and zoom are drawn by `StadiumComplexView`; they are not baked into the images. Construction images never modify finance or upgrade state.
