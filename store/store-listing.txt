APP NAME
BOSS XI

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Manage your football club, build your squad and take charge on matchday.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Take charge of a football club and make it yours. Pick your country and division, put together a starting XI and lead your team from the opening fixture to the final whistle.

• Build a squad: scout players across the database, compare ability and potential, and negotiate signings.
• Set your tactics: choose a formation, pick your starters and adjust your game plan at half-time.
• Follow the action: watch matches unfold on the live pitch, read commentary and review each player's contribution.
• Run the club: manage the budget, improve training facilities, and personalise your stadium stands.
• Play for more than one season: compete for promotion and trophies across domestic and continental competitions.

Core careers and matches are free. An optional, one-time Game Editor purchase lets you rename clubs and change team colours across your careers. The game uses alternate club identities and is an independent football management simulation.

RELEASE NOTES
Start a career, build a squad and manage matches across ten countries. The optional Game Editor can be unlocked through a one-time Google Play purchase. Back up or restore careers from Career Hub.
