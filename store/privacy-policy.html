Careers and settings

BOSS XI uses the manager name and date of birth you enter to create your manager profile. The game saves that profile, career progress, team edits and game settings in Android's private app storage. It does not require a BOSS XI account or send your career data to the developer. The game contains no advertising or developer analytics.

Optional editor purchase

Google Play processes payment through your Google account and provides the game with the product and transaction status needed to verify, acknowledge and restore access. BOSS XI does not see your payment card details, send receipts to the developer or include purchase receipts in exported saves. The Google Play Billing SDK and Play services may process transaction, device and diagnostic information to operate, protect and troubleshoot checkout. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles that information.

Backups and security

Android may back up or transfer app data based on your device and Google account settings. If you choose to export careers, Android's document picker lets you select the destination, which may be a cloud provider you use. Exports include your manager details and are not encrypted by BOSS XI, so keep them in a place you trust. The game does not upload exports to a developer service.

Retention and deletion

Career data and settings remain in app storage until you delete a career or clear the app's storage. To erase all local game data, clear storage for BOSS XI in Android Settings. Manage Android backups in your device settings and delete any exported copies from the storage location you chose. You can manage your Play order history with Google Play.
